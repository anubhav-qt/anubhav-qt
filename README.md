### Building

#### [Breader](https://github.com/anubhav-qt/breader)

An immersive e-reader for books and manga. I was building it for myself and kept adding things to it until it became a proper app.

It reads aloud with voices that run in your browser. It even has a panel by panel reading mode for manga. Every book fills up with its own colour as you read it to show your progress. No payments and no sign-up.

[breader.site](https://breader.site) · [Repo](https://github.com/anubhav-qt/breader)

#### [Jello](https://github.com/anubhav-qt/jello)

I was a drummer in school, played fingerstyle guitar for a few years and was very good at it, and picked up intermediate music theory along the way. So now I'm making my PC my instrument with Jello, and hopefully I'll make music of my own someday!

It's a desktop synth and an entire music studio you play on your PC's keyboard. Rust core, a C++ audio engine, and a TypeScript UI in Tauri. Still very early.

#### [Dodo](https://github.com/dodo-mojo/dodo)

A web framework for Mojo where your HTTP server, AI models, GPU kernels, computer vision and agents run in one runtime, in one language.

Pre-alpha, but the networking core works: HTTP/1.1, HTTP/2, TLS and WebSockets. The app API and CLI are next. Started on 26th October 2026, so it's very new.

#### [PariBelle](https://www.paribelle.in)

PariBelle is my family's fashion business. The storefront has been taking real orders since July 2026. POM handles the orders and operations, and my family and I use it every day. Then I added Seelie, an AI agent for the shop. It helps with shop operations, product photos, reels and Meta ads, everything from one interface and just using natural language.

Since September all of it runs on my ThinkPad, with Vercel, Render and Supabase as the fallback.

[paribelle.in](https://www.paribelle.in) · [POM](https://github.com/anubhav-qt/pom) · [Storefront](https://github.com/anubhav-qt/paribelle-web) · [Backend](https://github.com/anubhav-qt/paribelle-backend)

### Paused

#### [Spoin](https://github.com/anubhav-qt/spoin_bundle)

I started Spoin around the idea of scrolling through knowledge the same way you scroll through a social feed. You get one thing to learn, then another, with quizzes and progression so it doesn't just become an endless feed of random facts.

It grew into a curated knowledge corpus, a custom RAG implementation called FROG, and separate generation and verification layers. Cards are generated ahead of time, so the feed never waits for an LLM call.

Paused for now. My time is going to PDE (my full-time work), Anthology (my freelancing role), creating new projects like Jello and Dodo, and maintaining and upgrading Breader and PariBelle.
